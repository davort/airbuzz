## App name

AirBuzz

## Short description

Your Google Fitbit Air buzzes for the calls and messages you pick.

## Full description

Your Google Fitbit Air tracks your sleep, steps and heart rate. It just doesn't buzz when Mum calls. AirBuzz fixes that.

Pick the people whose calls should ring your Fitbit Air, and the apps whose messages should buzz it. Calls buzz every second until you answer, from the phone app, WhatsApp and Signal. Twenty messages in fifteen seconds buzz once. Everything else stays on your phone: no deals, no likes, no delivery updates.

Stay quiet at night, in Do Not Disturb, or while you're using the phone. The people you choose can still get through.

Before you install:
• Android 9 or later. Not on iPhone: iOS keeps other apps' notifications to itself.
• Google Health keeps syncing. AirBuzz borrows the band for each buzz, then hands it back.
• A buzz starts a few seconds after your phone rings.
• Nothing is ever uploaded. AirBuzz has no internet access and never stores message text.
• Free. No ads, no account, no subscription. Open source (GPL-3.0).
• Want every notification on your wrist? A watch suits you better.

AirBuzz is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with Google or Fitbit. It uses an unofficial protocol, so a firmware update could stop it working. Google, Fitbit and Google Fitbit Air are trademarks of Google LLC.
