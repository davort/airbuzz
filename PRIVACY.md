# AirBuzz privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026.

AirBuzz is published by Misek AB. For questions about this policy or your data, write to support@airbuzz.me.

## What AirBuzz keeps, and where

Everything AirBuzz keeps stays on your phone, in storage only AirBuzz can read. Android's backup and phone-to-phone transfer are turned off for AirBuzz, so none of it is copied to the cloud or to a new phone.

- **Alerts.** AirBuzz reads your notifications to recognise calls and messages, so it can make your Fitbit Air buzz. For each one it keeps the app, the caller or chat name, the app's ID for the chat, the caller's number when the call shows one, and what it did (for example "buzzed" or "skipped during quiet times"). It keeps these for one week, then deletes them.
- **Message text.** AirBuzz never stores the text of a message.
- **The people you choose.** You pick people with Android's contact picker. AirBuzz keeps only their names and phone numbers. Unless you allow it to list contacts (below), it can't read the rest of your contacts.
- **Callers you hide from "Called recently"**: their number, or their name when there is none, so they stay hidden.
- **Your Fitbit Air's battery**: its last reading (the percentage, whether it was charging, and when), to show it in AirBuzz.
- **Your settings**, such as the apps you choose, quiet times, the vibration strength and the look of the app.
- **Chats.** For apps that name their chats (such as WhatsApp, Telegram and Discord), AirBuzz keeps the name of each chat, person or group that sent you a notification, the app's ID for it and whether it is a group, so you can choose which chats buzz. It also keeps a small copy of the picture that chat's notification shows, to show it in that list. It forgets a chat, and its picture, after 30 days without a message. The chats you choose are kept with your settings.
- **Contacts, only if you allow it.** When you open an app's chats, AirBuzz asks whether to list your contacts who use that app, and you can say Not now. If you allow it, AirBuzz reads only the names and photos of your contacts who use WhatsApp or Telegram, to show them in that list. It doesn't copy or keep them; it reads them again each time you open the list. A contact you tick is kept by name with your settings, like a chat. You can take the permission back at any time in Android's settings.
- **A step log** that helps find out why an alert didn't buzz. It records which apps posted notifications and when, what AirBuzz did, timings, whether Bluetooth is on, the strength of the Bluetooth signal to your Fitbit Air, your phone's battery level and your Fitbit Air's. It never contains message text, caller names or numbers. It keeps one week of steps, and never more than the last 1,000.

## What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you send it yourself. AirBuzz has no permission to use the internet. It collects nothing and shares nothing with anyone, including its maker.

The step log leaves your phone only when you press **Share log** and choose where to send it, such as an email. The shared log also includes your phone's maker and model, its Android version and the AirBuzz version.

AirBuzz talks to your Fitbit Air only over Bluetooth. It sends your Fitbit Air only the command to vibrate, never what an alert says, and asks it for its battery level. To tell when Google Health is syncing, it counts how much the Fitbit Air sends Google Health. That traffic is encrypted, and AirBuzz can't read it.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling AirBuzz, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it kept.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here, with a new effective date.
